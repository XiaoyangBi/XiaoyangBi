### 🚀 About Me

I'm **XiaoyangBi (毕骁扬 / Billyy)**, an **AI Product Manager** and **researcher** exploring the deep integration of AI with real-world scenarios. Currently pursuing my Master's degree in **Automation (AI track)** at **Xiamen University (985)**, recommended for admission.

I have product experience at **Tencent (WeChat Pay & CSIG AI)**, working on WeChat Pay's "Withdrawal Savings" banking strategy and e-commerce search planning, as well as AI-powered industrial quality inspection. My research covers **Computer Vision, Diffusion Models, and GANs**, with publications at **IJCNN (CCF-C)** and a first-author paper submitted to **JIMS (SCI Q1)**.
